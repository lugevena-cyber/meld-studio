MELD bundles 30 MP3 recordings, copied without changing their audio files.
MELD's player repitches nearby keys, applies gain/envelopes/filtering and optional
room delay during playback. This is a compact single-layer browser bank; it does
not include the complete 16-velocity-layer SFZ library or its pedal/noise layers.
